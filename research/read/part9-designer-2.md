## The part

**Handovers.** Each holds. Chapter 22 closes on "It also uses interfaces, as a person does"; 23 on "An AI agent that operates an interface may also write one"; 24 hands Part X the gate.

**Contradictions.** None between two sentences. Two tensions: chapter 22 gives each rule "the article it derives from", and chapter 23 prints Rule 82 under two; chapter 22 says "level 1 checks that the file is well formed", and level 1 refuses an unknown verb (chapter 24) and an unknown role (chapter 22's court).

**Clearest plate:** 22.2 against 22.1. I restate it without looking: writer and actor changed, report did not. **Hardest:** 24.7: its point sits in one short line above fifteen syntax errors that say something else.

**Taken away.** When an AI agent acts or writes, a file answers three questions: a way back, a named authority on a proposal that acts, every held state shown. A person answers three more: who the named actor is, which role is true, and what "clean" means where nothing was decided. The "[AUTHOR: the stake ...]" line on each page I read as a marked hole.
