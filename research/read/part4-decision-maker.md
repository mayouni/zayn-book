# Part IV, read from the door of the decision-maker who commissions software

Reader: a fresh reader, the decision-maker's door, 2026-11-27. Pages: chapter 10, then chapter 11 (`.cache/as-read/`). Read twice, the second time with the brief's questions.

## Chapter 10, What a declaration cannot see

1. **What I remember.** A clean declaration certifies what the user is doing, not how the screen draws it: the same payment file runs clean under a careful drawing and under one with seven faults. Rules split in two, the declaration's half refused by the verifier (Rule 79's missing undo), the drawing's half left to the audit of the painted page and to a person.
2. **Where I lost the thread.**
   - "Zayn's audit reads the pages of one site, Zayn's own" (Where the field leads): I had read the audit as an instrument I could ask a supplier to run; here it reads only Zayn's site. The page never says whether it can read my product.
   - "Designed: ... LAYOUT_DISPLACEMENT, measured by the audit's K22" (Where it stands): the table files K22 under "the page in time: loading, acting, answering". A displacement measured by a time check reads as a mismatch.
   - "with the reason quoted above" (What I refused): the quote that follows, "Rule 79's dialog clause is a fact about a rendering", is not the one quoted above.
   - Terms before they are said: "the contract's note" (What I refused; said only in chapter 11), "the manifest of the suite" (The court), "the enforcement ladder" (Where it stands), "Article IV" on a finding, "a renderer that Softanza owns" (Softanza never said), "the platform", "toast", "both cultures" ("the two ways it draws" does not tell me it means two languages).
3. **Where I doubted.** "Zayn's constitution of 123 rules" sits beside "Rule 129, The Action Is the Last Thing to Go". With 123 rules, a Rule 129 needs one clause on the numbering. Where it stands gives its content twice, as a list and as a paragraph, word for word.
4. **What was mine.** What a supplier's clean report covers and what it does not. I got it, and plainly: "a clean declaration says that what a user is doing keeps every rule the verifier checks." The other half is missing. What I can require for the drawing today (the audit on my pages, or a person's review against which list), and when it can be checked ("after the fact"), is never put as something a buyer can ask for.
5. **Verdict.** `ready after the items above`. The line between declaration and drawing is the most useful thing in the book for a buyer, but the page stops before saying who checks my drawing. Stopped me most: "Zayn's audit reads the pages of one site, Zayn's own, against its own design system."

## Chapter 11, The strata of expression

1. **What I remember.** Between the intent and the drawing sits a layer of six functions (structure, signal, role, motion, message, tempo), each naming what an expression is for and never how it looks. Two of the six are the language's own fields with their own refusals (red refused for danger, button refused for reversible). Three are carried by older fields, and tempo is held in the law.
2. **Where I lost the thread.**
   - "TONE critical" (Plate 11.10): the exercise refuses `TONE danger` and expects me to find a lawful tone, but the page never gives the list of tones. "Chapter 7 runs that refusal" is a pointer back to a page I may not have read, and I need the list now.
   - "from a measurement of the sound plane" (Tempo): neither the measurement nor "the sound plane" is said.
   - "the reference design" (What I refused): not said.
   - Terms before they are said: "level 1" and "level 2" in the court (said in chapter 10, not here), "Article I" and "Article II" on every finding, "the platform" ("the founding architecture of the platform").
3. **Where I doubted.**
   - "Zayn's role names the consequence, and the verifier holds the control to it." (Where the field leads). Chapter 10 has just shown that the verifier holds the declaration, never the drawn control. The sentence claims more than both pages show.
   - The court lists ROLE_PROMISE_BROKEN, "A reversible control keeps no reachable UNDO." Plate 10.3 is exactly that, a reversible control with only CANCEL, and it got MISSING_UNDO alone. Two codes seem to cover one situation and only one fires. The page should say which applies when.
   - ROLE_PROMISE_BROKEN is the only fixture in the court without a lawful twin, and nothing says why.
4. **What was mine.** Which parts I can require of a supplier now and which are proposals awaiting a ruling. I got it from the table "Stratum | In the language | Stage", which I could restate without looking. I also learnt that a role cannot yet be required of each control: "requiring a role of each control waits for evidence". A supplier can omit roles and receive only a warning, which matters to a contract.
5. **Verdict.** `ready after the items above`. The stages are clear, but one sentence claims the drawn control is checked, and the exercise asks for a word it never gives. Stopped me most: "Zayn's role names the consequence, and the verifier holds the control to it."

## For the part

- **Hand-overs.** Chapter 10 ends on the words the language leaves to the drawing, and chapter 11 opens on them. Chapter 11 ends on "one declaration, carried in 10 channels", a clean bridge into Part V.
- **Contradictions.**
  - Chapter 10: "the verifier reads the declaration, and the audit of the painted page reads the drawing." Chapter 11: "the verifier holds the control to it." The second should say "the declaration".
  - "Zayn's constitution of 123 rules" (both openings) against Rule 125 (chapter 11) and Rule 129 (chapter 10).
  - Plate 10.3 gives MISSING_UNDO alone, while chapter 11's court gives ROLE_PROMISE_BROKEN for "a reversible control [that] keeps no reachable UNDO".
- **Clearest plate.** 10.2: the same file, word for word, clean under a drawing that the illustration marks with seven rules broken. It is the clause I would put in a call for tenders.
- **Hardest plate.** 11.9 with its repair 11.10: the reader has to produce a tone from a list neither page prints.
- **What my reader takes away.** A clean Zayn report from a supplier certifies the intent: verbs, undo, promises and meanings, checked before anything is drawn. It does not certify the screen. The screen answers to an audit (today run on Zayn's own site), five of whose checks meet the seven faults, and to a person for the rest. Of the expression layer, SIGNALS and ROLE are what I can require today, and roles only as a warning. What adoption costs is not on either page, and I came for that too.
