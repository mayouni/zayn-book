# Part III from one door: the decision-maker who commissions software

Chapters 6 to 9 as built, read in order on 2026-11-23. I changed nothing.

## Chapter 6 · Intent first

1. **Remember.** A supplier declares what a user does, in 22 fixed verbs, before any screen is drawn, and a build reads the exit code. Proof: NAVIGATE is refused under Article II, and an undeclared element under Rule 130.
2. **Lost the thread.** The court: "the same check one layer down, citing Article II and no rule". Terms never said: "layer"; "Set" and "Tier machine" (rule card); "inventory" beside "symbol table" for one file. "123 numbered rules", then "Rule 130".
3. **Doubted.** The principle says "never where a declaration starts"; WIDGET then gets "A warning, and the exit code is 0".
4. **Mine.** When the check happens: "Zayn checks what was declared before the drawing begins." Got it. Not got: who supplies the inventory in a project, and whether a build can stop on warnings.
5. **Verdict: ready after the items above.** Warnings that pass weaken what I can require. Stopped most: "A warning, and the exit code is 0: the language has no kind named WIDGET".

## Chapter 7 · Ten declarations, one grammar

1. **Remember.** Ten kinds and closed lists of words; a word outside them is refused from the file alone. Proof: COLOR, a misplaced TONE, a doubled field and "kiosk" each exit 1.
2. **Lost the thread.** A field it does not know: "the report has two lines where chapter 8's had three"; chapter 8 comes later. The principle: "The verifier's contract gives the reason in one clause"; "contract" is said only in chapter 8, and at my door it means my agreement with a supplier.
3. **Doubted.** "That is all of it."; no table says which field goes on which kind. "A file that carried one was accepted the day before": which version does a project pin?
4. **Mine.** Whether clean means checked. Got it, and the answer is no: "SCREN is not a kind of this language", exit 0. The page ends on a question where I needed a rule.
5. **Verdict: ready after the items above.** Clean is not yet proof that a file was read. Stopped most: "The first draft was clean because the verifier read nothing of it".

## Chapter 8 · The refusal

1. **Remember.** A refusal names a code, a declaration and a numbered rule, as a report, a machine form and an exit code. Proof: a payment approval gets MISSING_UNDO (Rule 79) and MISSING_CANCEL (Rule 26), then runs clean.
2. **Lost the thread.** Every finding, in one run: "it keeps that dialog for acts that cannot be undone"; the card of Rule 79 printed above does not say so. Title line: "Spine" is never said.
3. **Doubted.** "has two answers and no third"; the exercise shows a third, quoted under 5. Plate 8.8 passes once the wizard "no longer says that it acts"; nothing ties the act to it.
4. **Mine.** What a build reads: "0 when the verifier refused nothing, 1 when it refused, 2 when it could not read the file." Got it, with its limit: "A refusal covers what a declaration states". Not got: what holds the delivered screen to a clean declaration.
5. **Verdict: ready after the items above.** Its exit-code sentence can go into a requirement as written. Stopped most: "Adding UNDO silences MISSING_UNDO and makes the declaration untrue".

## Chapter 9 · One verdict, two verifiers

1. **Remember.** A verdict is fixed by 77 files with known answers, and a verifier states the level it can see. Proof: "pass 77 · fail 0", and one flow goes from clean to HIDDEN_AUTHORITY once a list of actors is given.
2. **Lost the thread.** A third evaluator: "four of them added on 8 November 2026 for the menu fixtures and written by expectation". Never said: "forged", "its judge", "Cross-pillar", "The gate".
3. **Doubted.** Built: "held to the JavaScript reference byte for byte"; that run is recorded for 0.1.0, and for 0.2.0 only "the Windows file passing the suite". The runner prints "conforms at level 4"; the page says "The suite holds no fixture above level 3".
4. **Mine.** What clean certifies: "nothing was refused among the checks the table made possible". Got it. The table is "written by hand", so by my supplier; the page does not draw that consequence.
5. **Verdict: ready after the items above.** For release 0.2.0 the page records less than "built" claims. Stopped most: "the files for the other systems as built".

## The part

**Hand-over.** 6 to 7 and 7 to 8 hold, though chapter 8 explains the exit code and //--> again. 8 to 9 promises a run that 9 tells from records.

**Contradictions.**
- Ch. 8: "gives the same declarations to two verifiers, the reference and the command". Ch. 9: the command and the others "are told from their own records".
- Ch. 6, on WIDGET: "the verifier reports the word where it is written". Ch. 7, on SCREN: "a kind of another language is passed over".
- Ch. 6: "A flow is the one kind of declaration that the verifier asks for its SATISFIES." Ch. 7: "the two fields every kind carries".

**Clearest plate:** 8.1, with its two dashed controls and their rules. **Hardest:** 8.8: where did the wizard's act go?

**What I take away.** I can require declarations in a closed vocabulary, a check that exits 0, 1 or 2 before anything is drawn, a numbered rule under each refusal, and a stated level. I cannot yet require that the delivered screen keeps what a clean declaration promised, nor trust "clean" without knowing what was read and which table was given. None of the four pages says what adoption costs: who writes the declarations, and which of the five version numbers a contract pins.
