# Part VIII, The Commons, read from the door of the builder of tools

Pages read in order: chapter 20 (From a remark to a rule), chapter 21 (Core, commons, local). Read once without notes, then again
with the brief's questions. Read on 2026-11-27.

## Chapter 20: From a remark to a rule

**What I remember.** The law's newer rules come from a corpus of 489 corrections kept as data, each with words, cause, date and two
marks (verbatim, repeat). The proof is one record followed to Rule 112 and its refusal DECORATIVE_ANIMATION, run on the page with
its fixtures; two other records end at a rule a person reads (106) and an amendment that waits (105).

**Where I lost the thread.**
- "What a record keeps": "A record whose source holds only a session's wording is a paraphrase, and the field says which." Which
  field? The record's fields are listed in prose, never by the names they have in the data. Chapter 21 gives `derived_from` by name;
  this page names no field and no file, so I cannot read the corpus with my own tool from what it says.
- "Two records and a cause": "Rule 106 is filed under machine-later, the tier of a rule..." and "its lint field is empty". Both
  terms arrive in a clause, which works, but "tier" here means an enforcement tier; chapter 21 opens by defining "tier" as core,
  commons, local. Two meanings of one word on facing pages.
- "The capture, designed": "an operator meets a pain, in the app, at the moment it hurtscapture: the surface..." The two parts of
  the diagram run together as text, and "operator" is the record's word where the book's is "user".

**Where I doubted.**
- Plate 20.4 changes two fields at once (TONE celebratory, MOTION glide); 20.5 changes TONE as well as MOTION and REPORTS. I cannot
  tell from the plates whether TONE takes part in the check. The page states the predicate once, loosely: "The verifier checks that
  a motion reports." I need it exactly: is it refused when MOTION is not `none` and REPORTS is absent? Is `MOTION none` with no
  REPORTS clean? Plate 20.6 tells me a glide that reports passes, which is useful and honestly said.
- "the menu remark of 8 November had its checks the same day" beside "Rule 116 gathered five records over three months before its
  checks were written". Both can be true (the last remark, then the checks), but read together they seem to time the same loop two
  ways.

**What was mine.** The data shape of a record, so a second tool could read or write one. I got the fields as a list of meanings and
the counts (235 verbatim, 174 repeat, 217 merged, 26 set apart), not the names, the file or the values a field takes. Partly got.

**Verdict.** Ready after the items above. The sentence that stopped me most: "A record whose source holds only a session's wording
is a paraphrase, and the field says which."

## Chapter 21: Core, commons, local

**What I remember.** The proposal designs four tiers (statute, core, commons, local) with one order: a local law may be stricter,
never looser. Built today: the law's parts as data, the pack of 12 files pinned by hash, a team's own contract via `--contract`, and
VIBE_STRIPS_RIGHT, run on the page, which keeps a culture from hiding a verb another declaration guarantees.

**Where I lost the thread.**
- "What a team holds today": "each recorded with its hash in a manifest". Which hash, and what does the manifest look like? A
  second implementation of the pack's check needs the algorithm and the file list. "Chapter 25 tells the pack's command" points
  forward to the command, not to the format.
- Plate 21.3: "SURFACE_AS names a medium outside the ten this language defines." The ten are not listed here and the page does not
  say where they are; to fix the exercise I had to guess that `visual` is one.

**Where I doubted.**
- After Plate 21.1: "The undo is a right of the core, and the place may not take it away." The run shows less: the check refuses a
  VIBE that suppresses a verb another declaration lists in ALWAYS_REACHABLE. Plate 21.2 is clean because "zoom was promised by no
  one". If the flow had not listed UNDO, would suppressing it pass? The page does not say; the sentence claims the core's right, the
  check holds the declared promise. State the claim at the size of the check.
- Citations differ in shape from chapter to chapter: DECORATIVE_ANIMATION cites "Rule 112 · ... · Article I"; VIBE_STRIPS_RIGHT cites
  "Article VI" alone; UNKNOWN_MEDIUM cites nothing. A builder writing a report reader needs to know that a finding cites a rule and
  article, an article alone, or nothing, and why. One sentence would settle it.

**What was mine.** Whether I could plug my own tool in, and what conformance asks. I got the most here: `derived_from` by name, 77
fixtures, the contract's three properties (severity, level, citation), and a run of the contract made both stricter and looser with
the exit codes, said honestly ("The verifier follows the contract a team gives it"). The closing line, "an implementation that adds a
verb is not a superset of Zayn, it is a different language that fails the fixtures", is the conformance rule I came for. Got, short
of the manifest's format.

**Verdict.** Ready after the items above. The sentence that stopped me most: "The undo is a right of the core, and the place may not
take it away."

## For the part

**Hand-over.** Chapter 20 ends "The next chapter takes the tiers a rule passes through", and chapter 21 begins with exactly that.
Chapter 20's capture section says "Chapter 21 tells the tiers that loop runs through"; chapter 21 points back: "chapter 20 tells its
first step". It hands over both ways.

**Contradiction.** One, of words: chapter 20, "machine-later, the tier of a rule whose instrument is to come"; chapter 21, "A tier
of the law is the level at which a rule is written and kept". Two kinds of tier, one word, no sentence that keeps them apart. A
builder mapping the data would look for one field called tier and find two ideas.

**Clearest plate.** 21.1 and 21.2 as a pair: one line changes (UNDO to ZOOM), the finding names the line (`crew.zayn:7`), the verb
and the VIBE, and I could write the check from it. **Hardest.** 20.4 with 20.5: two fields change at once, so the plates do not
isolate what the check reads.

**What this reader takes away.** I could restate what a team holds today without looking: a pin by hash, a contract passed with
`--contract`, a culture limited by one refusal, closed lists of verbs and media. I could write the admission test as a function
over `derived_from`. I could not yet write a reader for the corpus or a second pack check, because the corpus fields and the
manifest's format are not named on these pages.
