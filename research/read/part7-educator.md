## The part

**Handovers.** 16 to 17: "The next chapter reads them."; 17 counts the tiers and defines one. 17 announces promotion; 18 tells none. 18 to 19 is clean.

**Contradictions.**
- "Correction" names three things. Chapter 18: "If the answer is no, the change is a correction" (wording); "A correction that was already law" (a person's remark); "the corrections the project made to itself" (the ledger). Chapter 19: "Zayn calls such a remark a correction".
- Chapter 17: "Every rule filed under machine, and most filed under tool, name in their lint entry the instrument". Chapter 19: "the pointer is written when the instrument exists". Yet chapter 17's table names no instrument for five of the 11 machine rules.

**Clearest plate:** 16.1: its rule, article and right each found in a table on the page. **Hardest:** 17.5.

**Takeaway.** The order holds for a class: structure, enforcement, change, authoring. Each page assumes Part III's grammar (kinds, fields, verbs, levels); the four-word door covers reading, not exercises. The real exercises are 18's and 19's menus; 16's needs the media chapters, 17's a field never shown; every one prints its answer in `//-->` lines above "write the codes you expect".
