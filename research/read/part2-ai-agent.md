4. **What was mine.** Exact rules for four codes, each with its trigger in the contract's words (e.g. "SATISFIES holds SELECT and ACT without CONFIRM"). Got it. I could write each offending and lawful pair without looking.
5. **Verdict:** ready after the items above. Stopped most: "The verifier checks Rules 82, 79, 26, 51 and 16 by name".

## Chapter 4, The instruments of the field

1. **What I remember.** Each instrument of the field (criterion, token, story, linter, screenshot, CLS) reads one object; a declaration is another object, and two drawings alike can carry different promises. Proof: SELECT_ACT_COLLAPSE and ROLE_ACTS_WITHOUT_CONSEQUENCE refused and repaired; a clean run at `--level 4`.
2. **Lost the thread.** Plate 4.6 (INTERACTION, SATISFIES [SELECT, ACT], no ALWAYS_REACHABLE) gets MISSING_UNDO but no MISSING_CANCEL, though chapter 3's contract line reads "A flow that acts cannot be abandoned: ALWAYS_REACHABLE lacks CANCEL." Plates 4.4 and 4.8 act with no CANCEL and run clean. I infer MISSING_CANCEL applies to INTENT_FLOW only and MISSING_UNDO to both; no page says which construct each check reads. "ROLE": three values named (inert, navigate, reversible); chapter 3 points to "a control that declares its act final". Is ROLE's list closed, and is "final" a fourth value?

Both hold, but an agent reading a clean level-4 report needs the sentence that says level 4's one kind is not run by the verifier. The closing overstates: "a clean report at level 4 says that the declaration keeps the law"; chapter 3 says "a person judges the others". It keeps the rules the verifier checks.
4. **What was mine.** ROLE's promise as data, and the finding when SATISFIES contradicts it. Got it for inert and navigate; the message "promising to change nothing" for a navigate tile is reconciled by the contract quotation, which helps.
5. **Verdict:** ready after the items above. Stopped most: "A control promising to change nothing declares that it acts." (printed for a ROLE navigate, plate 4.6).

2. **Lost the thread.** The ten media and the three carriage grades are named ("natively, at a cost, or not at all"); only signage's three verbs and two of print's are given. A forward pointer would do; none is given.
3. **Doubted.** VERB_NOT_CARRIED cites "Rule 82 · Perceivable State · Article II"; chapter 3's INVISIBLE_STATE cites "Rule 82 · Perceivable State · Article III", and its map files Rule 82 under Article III. See the contradiction below. 5. **Verdict:** ready after the items above. Stopped most: "Rule 82 · Perceivable State · Article II".
