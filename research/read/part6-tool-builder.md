# Part VI, The Readers: read from the door of the builder of tools

Pages read, in order, twice: chapter 14 (Two cultures, one law), chapter 15 (Culture as an axis). Read as the builder of a linter or a
second verifier: what data, what contract, what levels, what conformance asks.

## Chapter 14: Two cultures, one law

1. **What I remember.** A VIBE may set density, medium, motion and what motion reports, and may SUPPRESS verbs only when no
   declaration in the same file keeps them ALWAYS_REACHABLE; otherwise VIBE_STRIPS_RIGHT, citing Article VI and no rule. The proof is
   plates 14.1 and 14.2 and the court's kiosk fixture, compared to the manifest as sets.
2. **Where I lost the thread.**
   - "A culture changes how an interface looks..." then the report: the location convention. VIBE_STRIPS_RIGHT and DECORATIVE_ANIMATION
     report `invoices.zayn:13`, the line of `DEFINE VIBE retro`, while SUPPRESSES is on line 17 and MOTION blink on line 16; FIELD_MISPLACED
     (14.3) reports line 3, the TONE line. Which line a finding carries is not said; my implementation would disagree with yours.
   - "In your hands": DECORATIVE_ANIMATION appears in 14.4 but not in "The codes above, in the words of the verifier's contract"; its level
     is not given.
   - The value sets: DENSITY `comfortable`, `compact`; MOTION `none`, `blink`. The page never lists what DENSITY or MOTION accept.
   - 14.6: `REPORTS [invoice_state]` names nothing declared, and the file is clean. Whether REPORTS references are resolved is unsaid.
3. **Where I doubted.**
   - Tier contradiction on one page: "Rule 112, on motion, is a person's to judge" against the report line "Rule 112 · Motion Reports,
     It Never Decorates" on an error. If Rule 112 is judgment, which part of it does the machine enforce?
   - "lets a compiler check that no culture has quietly dropped a right": larger than the check, which reads one file; the page says so
     two sentences later ("a culture kept in a file of its own answers clean"), which I value. Put the scope in the same sentence.
4. **What was mine.** The scope of the check (one file), the citation (Article VI, no rule), one finding per suppressed verb (14.4 gives
   two VIBE_STRIPS_RIGHT), and "the run compares the two sets": I got the conformance rule. I did not get the location rule.
5. **Verdict: ready after the items above.** The sentence that stopped me most: "The site's own design system forbids blinking in both
   of its cultures, and Rule 112, on motion, is a person's to judge."

## Chapter 15: Culture as an axis

1. **What I remember.** SIGNALS accepts only Rule 118's five statuses, so a colour word is refused once per value (UNKNOWN_SIGNAL, level 1)
   and a declaration can never carry a colour; the rendering is per medium and culture. Rules 56, 64, 122 and the flag pattern are data,
   judged by a person or waiting for an instrument; plate 15.3 runs clean and is still unlawful.
2. **Where I lost the thread.**
   - "Rule 118's second family, the quiet one" (In your hands): is `muted` one of the five statuses or the second family? Line 42 lists
     muted among the five; chapter 14 says "the five status colours and the neutral one".
   - UNKNOWN_SIGNAL is "level 1" in the contract list, yet the run is "at level 2". That levels include those below is not said on these
     pages.
   - 15.4: EXPRESSION_ORPHAN, "An expression field is declared on a declaration that satisfies no intent." Which fields are expression
     fields? In 15.3, `lang_fr` carries LABEL and no SATISFIES, and is clean; so LABEL is not one. I cannot draw the list.
   - 15.3: `TARGET french_pages` names nothing declared, and the file is clean; same question as REPORTS.
3. **Where I doubted.**
   - "The verifier decides one thing on this axis: the colour word" while the court of the same page runs SIGNAL_UNPERCEIVABLE.
   - "Zayn leads in what the declaration refuses to carry": stated at the right size by the plates; not an advertisement.
4. **What was mine.** The closed set for SIGNALS, one finding per bad value, the tiers named (judgment, machine-later), and the honest
   line "the suite holds no fixture for them". For a second implementation I know exactly what this axis asks of a verifier, and that
   the rest is not mine to automate. I got it.
5. **Verdict: ready after the items above.** The sentence that stopped me most: "Is a price that rose a status... or a fact with no
   urgency, in Rule 118's second family, the quiet one?"

## For the part

- **Hand-over.** Chapter 14 closes on "the culture the reader brings" and 15 opens on it; 14's last section ("The law a reader's language
  keeps") seeds 15. Clean.
- **Contradictions.**
  - Chapter 14: "the five status colours and the neutral one" against chapter 15: "success, warning, danger, info and muted" as Rule 118's
    five. A builder reads six against five unless the neutral one is the second family; say it.
  - Chapter 14: Rule 64 "says what never turns: clocks, the controls of a player, the digits of a number"; chapter 15 quotes the list with
    "any progress that encodes a physical direction". Not false, but the first reads as the whole list.
  - Chapter 14, "Rule 112, on motion, is a person's to judge", against its own plate 14.4 refusing with Rule 112 cited.
- **Clearest plate:** 15.1 then 15.2: two refusals, one per value, then the meaning words; I could write the check from them.
- **Hardest plate:** 14.4: three findings on one line that is neither the MOTION line nor the SUPPRESSES line, one code outside the
  page's contract list, and a rule the page calls a person's.
- **What the builder takes away.** Two machine checks with their scope (VIBE_STRIPS_RIGHT per file, UNKNOWN_SIGNAL per value), a
  set-based conformance rule, and a clear line between what a verifier enforces and what the law leaves to a person. Missing for a
  second implementation: the rule for a finding's line, whether levels are cumulative, the value sets of VIBE's fields, which fields are
  expression fields, and whether names in REPORTS and TARGET must resolve.
