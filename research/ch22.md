## 4. The principle

The sentence the record returns to is the law's own: "An intelligent agent is an actor under this law — never an authority above
it." (`constitution/rules.json:43`), said again in the set's description, "Same norms, same audit, same dignity."
(`:2451`). The contract gives Rule 98 its short form: "Intelligence advises; execution decides." (`conformance/violations.json:407`).
And the generate page gives the division of labour: "Every refusal of meaning stays with the court." (`site/generate.html:110`).

Proposals for the chapter's one bold sentence, in the book's words (the author's to rule):

> **An AI agent acts under the same law as any user, and a declaration is judged by what it states, whoever wrote it.**

> **An AI agent may propose and may act; the law judges what is declared, and a named authority answers for what commits.**

What it means, to say after it: the verifier reads fields, not writers (block 1); the law asks every act to name its actor, and
an AI agent is named like a person (blocks 1 and 3); where the law distinguishes an AI agent from a person (Rule 98's avoid list,
Rule 102), a person reads the names (block 4; chapter 17).
