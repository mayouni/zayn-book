## 1. What the chapter does

It takes the field's instruments one by one (written criteria such as WCAG's success criteria; design systems and their
tokens; component catalogues such as Storybook; linters of interface code; audit engines that read the painted page, such as
axe-core; visual and regression tests) and says, for each, what it reads (the code, the tokens, the rendered component, the
painted page) and what it decides. It then runs Zayn plates to show that a declaration of what a user is doing is a different
object: a screen whose selecting acts, and a card that promises to change nothing and acts, are refused for what they let a
user do, which nothing the field's instruments read carries. And it shows the other half: a clean declaration leaves to the
audit of the painted page what only a drawing shows (Rule 38's displacement, the contract's one level-4 kind). The law itself
names, rule by rule, which instrument answers for it (the `lint` field of `rules.json`), and the chapter can read that field
as the bridge between the field's instruments and the declaration.

## 4. The principle

The record returns to one sentence: Article II's "widgets are consequences of intent, never primitives"
(`constitution/rules.json:27`). The instruments of the field read widgets and their drawing; the declaration reads the intent.
Its other half is the contract's note on the one level-4 kind: "undecidable from a declaration. Measured today by
tools/ui-audit.html, which is the enforcement ladder working as designed." (`conformance/violations.json:136`).

Proposed bold sentence, in the book's words: **An instrument of the field reads how an interface is drawn; a declaration says
what a user is doing, and the law refuses it for that.** (A second, for the closing of the section on the audit: *What only
a drawing shows is left to the instrument that reads the drawing.*)

## 11. Terms a designer who does not program needs

- **Instrument**: anything that reads an interface and decides something about it; the law's tiers speak of an instrument
  that "is invented" (`doc/THE-SECOND-FOUNDING.md:33`) and its rules name one in a `lint` field (`rules.json`).
- **Success criterion**: a testable statement of WCAG a page meets or fails (`site/practices.html:91`; F-17).
- **Design system**: guidelines, components and tools for building an interface, applied by people (`site/practices.html:100`).
- **Design token**: a named value, such as a colour or a size, kept as data so tools share it (`site/practices.html:106`;
  F-12); Zayn's own: `doc/design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md:594-596`.
- **Component catalogue (Storybook)**: a place to build, show, test and document components apart from the application
  (`site/practices.html:112`).
- **Linter**: a tool that checks source code against rules (chapter 1's definition; F-04).
- **Audit of the painted page**: a tool that opens a page in a browser and measures what is drawn (`tools/ui-audit.html:7`).
- **Painted page / the drawing**: the book's vocabulary (`CLAUDE.md` of the book).
- **Level 4, projection**: the conformance level that needs "a rendered surface" (`doc/language/README.md:129`).
- **Layout shift**: content moving because something else appeared, grew or went (`doc/design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md:743`).
- **Role** (`inert`, `navigate`, `reversible`, `irreversible`): a control's promise about what it changes
  (`violations.json:369`; `doc/language/DECLARATIONS.md`, INTERACTION).
