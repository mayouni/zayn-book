## 1. What the chapter does

It takes the six strata of expression that *The Second Founding* proposes (structure, signal, role, motion, message, tempo:
the layer between what a user is doing and how one medium draws it) and, for each, says what the document proposes and what
stage that stratum has reached at the pin. The data split them cleanly: `SIGNALS` and `ROLE` are built as expression fields
with their own diagnostics and fixtures; structure, motion and message are carried by fields that predate the founding
(`NAV_GRAPH` levels, `STEPS`, `MOTION`, `FEEDBACK`'s `TONE` and `REPORTS`) and are judged by older checks; tempo is a
proposal only, and the law already holds its first rule (Rule 125). It runs one offending declaration and its lawful twin per
built stratum, and it states the rule that binds the whole layer: every expression traces to an intent. It says who ratifies
(the author, section by section) and what the record shows him and his delegations adopting, holding and deferring.
Chapter 10 takes where a declaration's sight ends and the audit of the painted page; chapter 11 takes the strata.

## 4. The principle

The record returns to one sentence, written in the founding and carried word for word into the contract as a diagnostic's
invariant: "Every expression declaration traces to an intent, or it is an error." (`doc/THE-SECOND-FOUNDING.md:214`;
`conformance/violations.json:316`). Its companion: "Expression is not appearance." (`:208`).

Proposed bold sentence, in the book's words: **An expression says what a meaning, a promise or a motion is for, never how it
looks, and it always answers to something a user is doing.** (Shorter alternative: *Expression refines an intent; it never
stands beside one.*)

## 11. Terms a designer who does not program needs

- **Expression (a stratum of)**: how an intent is made perceivable, between what a user does and how a medium draws it; it
  names "a meaning, a promise, or a relation", never a pixel (`THE-SECOND-FOUNDING.md:204,208-210`).
- **Stratum**: one function of expression (structure, signal, role, motion, message, tempo); "The strata are functions. The
  media are channels." (`:220`).
- **Medium / channel**: where an expression is carried (visual, auditory, voice, …) (`:228`; `DECLARATIONS.md:238`).
- **Signal**: a meaning about a record, one of Rule 118's five (`signal.json:53-80`).
- **Role**: the promise a control makes about what happens when a user acts (`ROLE-DESIGN.md:62-63`).
- **Tone**: the register in which the system speaks (`DECLARATIONS.md:244-245`).
- **Report (`REPORTS`)**: the state a feedback or a motion tells the user about; without it, motion is decoration
  (`DECLARATIONS.md:193,209`).
- **Ratification**: the author's ruling, section by section, that turns a proposal into the project's design or law
  (`THE-SECOND-FOUNDING.md:3-4`; `AMENDMENT-DOCKET.md:34-35`).
- **Orphan (`EXPRESSION_ORPHAN`)**: an expression on a declaration that satisfies no intent (`violations.json:310`).
