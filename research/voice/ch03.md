# Voice reading: chapter 3, What the field wrote

Reader: a fresh voice reader (`writing-framework/briefs/voice-reader.md`), 2026-11-25. Nothing was run; nothing in the chapter was changed.

1. **Shape (C9).** The opening keeps the form: first person, says what the chapter does, first sentence names the subject ("The field of human-computer interaction wrote down what a good interface does", opening). Two glossary paragraphs follow (six words defined, tiers listed) before the first section. That delays the start. Section closes: "A way back" ends on a strong hand-over ("What Zayn adds, in the site's words: 'Refusal.'"). "One meaning" ends on a verdict that reads ("the meaning is the declaration's"). "Interest before commitment" stops at a bare "Clean." with nothing after it. The chapter closes on homework, then a bridge ("The next chapter reads them: the instruments of the field."). It does not end on a summary.

2. **Run, then read (C1).** About 800 words come before the first plate. The nine-row map of rule numbers and tiers sits under "The principle", before any declaration has run. Worst passage (The principle): "A kind of finding binds Rules 82, 79, 26, 51 and 16, and the list of statuses in Rule 118". It comes before `SATISFIES` or a finding has been shown. "One fact sets the size of this chapter" (The principle) is also all abstraction. After that, the sections run first and read after. "Try to declare the size of a target." (Where the painted page judges) is the model.

3. **The defect (C2).** The exercise is a real defect: three findings in one file, and the repair is run. The question the run leaves is the best thing in the chapter: "can a payment be undone?" (In your hands). It tells the reader the printed repair is not the last word. Every section shows the wrong version before the right one. One taste reason is given and quoted: "Principles that are taste, naming, or product strategy stay in their projects." Form: the exercise fence lacks the `exercise` flag that the book's CLAUDE.md asks for. Its `//-->` promise lines show the three codes the reader is asked to predict. The repaired plate follows at once, under "Try before you read on." The rule for Parts I and V says the answer folds under a button.

4. **Where it stops (C3).** I found no line that undersells, and none of the gate's phrases ("not yet", "nobody has", "Limit:"). Lines that are negative in form but report a fact:
   - "Refused, and no rule is cited: the grammar holds no field for a target's size" (Where the painted page judges)
   - "The law keeps one rule of undo, and no rule of confirmation beside it." (What I refused, a heading in the negative). It could say what the law keeps: "One rule of undo, and its reason".
   - "a table that a team writes by hand today" (Where the field leads). This one is affirmative and states the stage.

   Claims that sound larger than the page shows:
   - "Clean: the verifier refuses a navigation that hides what the system knows, before anything is drawn" (State the user must see). This holds at level 3 with a table, and the paragraph above says level 2 is clean.
   - "in every file the verifier reads" (Where the field leads). Same reach: only where the table is given.
   - The court "holds the refusals run on this page", then lists missing-cancel, which no plate runs.

   "Designed: the expression fields proposed in the second founding": the page never says what "the second founding" is.

5. **The particulars (C4).**

   - One `[AUTHOR: …]` marker (the stake). It sits under "What I refused", where it reads as a fourth refusal. Give it its own place before "The court".

6. **Metaphors (C5).**
   - Carry weight: "gulf" (the field's own term, quoted); "the painted page" (the book's fixed term); "the court is silent exactly where the deterioration lives" (quoted from the ledger).
   - Unmapped sustained image: "ground", nine times ("the field's ground", "Fitts's ground", "Hick's ground", "Tufte's ground", "whose ground it keeps"). It is never mapped. Either name it once or let it go, since "subject" would do.
   - Decorative but apt: "the pick pays" and "A choice that pays".
   - The ruling metaphor (law, article, docket, ruled, unconstitutional, court) keeps its names stable.

7. **Address (C6).** "I", about 12: reads ("I read four of its sources"), runs, maps ("as I read it", twice), refuses. Most are in their right jobs. "We": none. "You", about 6: the reader who opens here, the imperative "Try to declare", and the exercise. One question ("can a payment be undone?"), answered in the next sentence: a beat. No exclamations. The tool is never quoted as speaking. "The verifier's messages say operator" reports its words. The pay plate's `ACTOR operator` against "In words: A user selects" is a small clash with the chapter's word.

8. **Rhythm and noise (C7).** Long then short is strong: "Clean." five times and "Refused." four times, as verdicts after a run. Colons throughout; no dashes. No intensifiers in the author's voice ("always", "carefully" are in quotes). Absolutes: "never given again", "never a colour", "every other word is said where it first appears". Awkward or calqued phrasing, three examples:
   - "Feedback in time, Shneiderman's informative feedback, is Rule 18's" (State the user must see)
   - "its lawful twin on this page is the book's" (The court), which is opaque
   - "Where a kind of finding binds that rule" (opening), where "binds" is record jargon used before it is explained

   No forbidden words.

9. **Counted sets (C8).** These match:
   - four sources (Nielsen, Shneiderman, the NN/g page on the gulfs, Tufte)
   - four words, then two more
   - four tiers, named exactly
   - three more pieces (Fitts, Hick, Gestalt)

Six expression fields are named with no count, which is fine. The court calls Rule 26 "one of the three rules the site sets beside control and freedom". The site sets control and freedom *and* error prevention beside them, so the attribution is off by one heuristic.

10. **The stake (C10).** The page rises at "What Zayn adds, in the site's words: 'Refusal.'" and at "The law leads in force". Both are backed by the runs above them. The chapter's own stake is the author's, and it is marked.
