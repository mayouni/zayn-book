## 1. What the chapter does

It opens the file that holds the law, `constitution/rules.json`, and reads it part by part for a designer who does not program:
the metadata and the version, the preamble, the philosophy and the axioms, the articles, the sets, the rules, the rights with the
rules that enforce each, the forbidden patterns with the rules each names, the retired and the burned numbers. It gives the anatomy
of one rule, field by field, and prints one rule whole. It shows that the law is data: every count on the page is read from the
file, and a refusal cites the file by number, by article key and by right. Three declarations are refused under three articles and
three rights, and each citation is followed into the data.

## 13. Hazards

- **The task's list of fields is two short.** It names `invariant`, `lint`, `vibes` as the fields only some rules carry; the
  data also holds `see_also` (7 rules) and `absorbs` (5). The anatomy needs all five, and `absorbs` is what ties a rule to a
  retired number.
- **No tier count as settled.** The census of the `enforcement` field (11, 7, 25, 80) may be printed only as a census of a
  field's values, through the tokens `{{n:law.tier.…}}`. No sentence says what a machine refuses. Sentences a ruling would
  change: the anatomy's line for `enforcement`; the tier word shown in the rule printed whole (`tool` for Rule 105, `machine`
  for Rule 79, `judgment` for Rule 112); any gloss of `lint` as "the check that runs".
- **Rule 105 and WCAG.** Its numbers (4.5:1, 7:1) will be recognized. The record's line is that the rules "do not cite WCAG"
  (`site/practices.html:92`): the chapter states no conformance and no borrowing.
- **`evidence` on the page.** TOKEN-01 says a rule's `evidence` is not printed for now. Printing one rule whole prints one
  evidence line: it is the author's call, and section 2k is what he needs to make it. The same list decides Appendix A: 22 values
  can be printed as they stand, 101 cannot, and names outside `evidence` need the same care (2k, last paragraph).
- **"This site", "the reader", "the framework's own site"** in evidence lines mean the site and the Reader of the law's first
  home. Printed in a book they read as the Zayn site or as the person reading.
- **Names.** The lineage, `intents.note`, the title of Set 10, the description of Set 30, Rule 44's `lint`, Rule 50's title and
  three rules' mention of a store hold names the book does not print. A quotation from these places is cut before the name or
  given in the book's words. The record's "the Principal" is "the author".
- **Two philosophies, three invariants.** The chapter says once which `philosophy` it means (the axioms in the metadata, the
  three principles in their own part) and uses "invariant" for the mark on a rule only, with "the family's sentence" for the
  other.
- **A designer reads the file and never writes it.** "Never asked to write JSON." (`doc/THE-SECOND-FOUNDING.md:864`). The
  exercise repairs a declaration; it asks for no edit of the law.
- **What the build needs before this chapter can be drafted** (the book's own tools, not a request to Zayn): `law_facts` in
  `tools/book_common.py` has no key for the retired and the burned numbers, the principles, the axioms, the rules per optional
  field, the entries of the lineage; and the builder has no table for the sets, the rules per article, the rights with their
  lists, the patterns with theirs, the retired numbers, nor a card that prints a rule whole. `{{right:id}}` prints the title and
  the statement and not `enforced_by` (`tools/build_book.py:687-694`). Typing any of these would break TOKEN-01.
- **Block 3b** needs `level=3 symbols=orders.json` and a `symbols` fence; its report line has no line number; its lawful form is
  the book's own. **The exercise** promises one code four times for one declaration: RUN-01 counts each.
- **The suite was not run** for this research. The court's box comes from the build.
