## 1. What the chapter does

It argues the deterioration from one data file: 489 corrections the author made to what a person sees, touches or hears,
each with its words, its place, its date and the cause that produced it. It counts them by script and says how (how many
quote him, how many name an earlier correction of the same defect, the 13 causes in 5 groups), follows three chains in
which a repair moved the defect instead of stopping it, and shows the Zayn site corrected under its own law, remark by
remark, each remark becoming a check of the painted page. Where a correction can be stated as a declaration, the verifier
refuses it on the page (motion that reports nothing; a filter the user cannot see); where it is a fact of the painted page,
the chapter names the audit's check and its recorded run. Its claim: the defects are the defaults of a generator, and a
default comes back until a rule names it and an instrument checks every case. It hands over to Part II, what the field
wrote. Chapter 19 already follows the menu remark (Z170, Z171) to a rule: this chapter shows the corpus whole and other
chains.
