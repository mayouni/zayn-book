## 1. What the chapter does

It states exactly where a declaration's sight ends. It takes chapter 8's payment approval, clean, and shows the record's own
demonstration: two drawings of one declaration, one humane and one breaking seven rules, both clean even at level 4, because
the language by design names no widget, no layout and no colour. It then draws the boundary rule by rule: for Rule 79 and
Rule 119 the declaration carries one half (that an undo exists, that a control which acts does not promise to change nothing)
and the drawing the other (where the undo sits, whether the control looks like a card); the corpus's causes fall on each side;
the audit's numbered checks read what is left, the painted page and the site's files. It shows the boundary moving, in both
directions: a menu's structure moved into the declaration by the author's ruling, and a rule half the audit cannot decide
until a declaration names what it must look for. It ends where chapter 11 begins: the strata the Second Founding designs for
the middle.

## 4. The principle

The record returns to one boundary, stated three ways. The language's: "No `WIDGET`, no layout, no colour. A declaration that
needs to say "card" has stopped describing intent." (`doc/language/DECLARATIONS.md:315-316`). The design pass's, on one rule:
"the declaration cannot see the defect … those are the same declaration with two renderings" (`doc/ROLE-DESIGN.md:130-133`).
And the contract's: "undecidable from a declaration" (`conformance/violations.json:136`).

Proposed bold sentence, in the book's words: **A declaration says what a user is doing, never how it is drawn; so one
declaration stands under many drawings, and a law that is broken only in the drawing passes it clean.** (Shorter variant:
*One declaration, many drawings: what differs between them, the declaration cannot see.*)

## 5. What I refused

From the record only.

- **A widget in the language.** `WIDGET` "is excluded on constitutional grounds"; admitting it "would let a declaration name a
  rendering and contradict the inversion" (`doc/language/README.md:168-171`). A session's document (the specification); the
  inversion it protects is Article II, the author's law.

  use the withdrawal itself.

  honoured. That silence was the defect" (`conformance/violations.json:434`). The contract's note; the record calls it a

  because "requiring an unproven vocabulary buys a declared role rather than an accurate one" (`conformance/violations.json:421`).

- **A fixture for the painted page.** "A fixture here would be theatre" (`conformance/manifest.json:593`). A session's
  document; chapters 4 and 17 quote it. Cite only.
- **A new rule for a column.** On the information-design harvest of 2026-08-15, candidate 3 was "Rejected as new law —

- **A menu's structure moved into the declaration**, the author's ruling of 2026-11-08 ("you must add this as an issue that is

## 11. Terms a designer who does not program needs

- **Declaration**: a file that says what a user is doing on a surface, and names no widget, layout or colour
  (`doc/language/DECLARATIONS.md:315`).
- **The drawing / the painted page**: what a medium shows of a declaration; the painted page is the drawing in a browser (the
  book's `CLAUDE.md`, vocabulary).
- **Rendering**: the record's word for a drawing in any medium (`DECLARATIONS.md:315-318`; `media.json:130`).
- **Level 4, projection**: the conformance level that needs "a rendered surface" (`doc/language/README.md:129`).
- **Symbol table**: the list of entities, actors and state a level-3 check is given, because a declaration cannot know them
  (`conformance/violations.json:13`).
- **The audit of the painted page** and **a check (K01 to K37)**: a page that opens another in a browser and measures what is
  drawn; each check reads one property of every element, at six widths, in two cultures (`DESIGN-SYSTEM.md:713-718`).
- **Root cause** (of a correction): "the default that produced the defect" (`DESIGN-SYSTEM.md:122`); chapter 2 defines it.
- **Role**: a control's promise about what it changes (`violations.json`, `ROLE_ACTS_WITHOUT_CONSEQUENCE`; chapter 4).
- **Undecidable**: what an instrument cannot settle from what it reads; the verifier stays silent on it, never passes it
  (`tools/zayn/README.md:61`).
- **Stratum** (for the bridge only): what an expression does, between intent and pixels (`THE-SECOND-FOUNDING.md:228-237`);
  chapter 11 defines it.
