## The author's claim, clause by clause

The claim (Second Founding, section 12): "a closed vocabulary, legislated by treaty, amended on crash evidence, working across
languages and literacy for sixty years".

| Clause | What the text carries | Where |
|---|---|---|
| legislated by treaty | Concluded at Vienna, 8 November 1968; in force 6 June 1978 "in accordance with article 39(1)"; 75 parties, 35 signatories | status page |
| a closed vocabulary | Where the convention prescribes a sign for a rule, parties undertake "not to use any other sign, symbol or marking for signifying that rule or conveying that information"; where it prescribes none, a party may use its own, "provided that such sign, symbol or marking is not assigned a different meaning in this Convention" and conforms to the system | Art. 3, 1 (a) (i), (ii) |
| (the classes) | Danger warning, regulatory (priority, prohibitory or restrictive, mandatory) and informative signs | Art. 5, 1 |
| (nothing confusable) | Parties prohibit any board or device "which might be confused with signs" | Art. 4 (b) |
| (old signs replaced) | Signs that do not conform are replaced within fifteen years of entry into force | Art. 3, 3 |
| working across languages | "In order to facilitate international understanding of signs", the system rests on "shapes and colours characteristic of each class of sign and, wherever possible, on the use of graphic symbols rather than inscriptions" | Art. 8, 1 |
| (and the reader who cannot read the words) | An inscription may stand on the sign "if this does not make the sign more difficult to understand for drivers who cannot understand the inscription"; inscriptions are in the national language or languages | Art. 8, 3 and 5 |
| amended | Any party may propose an amendment "accompanied by an explanatory memorandum"; it is deemed accepted unless a third of the parties reject it or ask for a conference within twelve months | Art. 41, 1 and 2 |
| on evidence | Parties may derogate "for experimental purposes and temporarily", "having regard to the usefulness of carrying out experiments before proposing amendments" | Art. 3, 1 (b) |
| amended in practice | The status page lists amendment notifications from 1994 onward, a proposal of 12 May 2025 (C.N.172.2025) and an acceptance of 22 May 2026 (C.N.170.2026) | status page |
| on crash evidence | **The text read does not say crash evidence.** It says experiments and an explanatory memorandum | Art. 3, 1 (b); Art. 41, 1 |
| literacy | **The text read does not name literacy.** It names drivers "who cannot understand the inscription" | Art. 8, 3 |
| for sixty years | Concluded 58 years before this reading; in force for 48 | status page |

## What chapter 5 may say as fact

The treaty, its classes, the closed vocabulary where it prescribes (and the conformity rule where it does not), shapes and
colours with symbols rather than words "to facilitate international understanding", amendments by the parties with a
memorandum, experiments before amendments, the dates and the count of parties. The author's "crash evidence", "literacy"
and "sixty years" are stated at the size the text carries: experiments, drivers who cannot read the inscription, since 1968.
