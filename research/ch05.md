## 1. What the chapter does

It reads road signage as an interface held by law: a fixed set of shapes, colours and symbols, set by treaty and amended,
which a driver reads at speed in any language. It says exactly what the treaty texts and the safety-sign standards carry,
once the writer has read them at their source and dated the reading (the record holds this claim at the level of the
scheme only). Then it shows Zayn making the same move: a closed vocabulary (the 22 verbs, the ten media, the five
semantic statuses), meanings assigned by rule, and the `signage` medium in Zayn's data, where the verifier refuses a
verb a sign cannot carry. It ends on the part the record has designed and not built: an external standard that
outranks the constitution in a physical medium.

## 4. The principle

The record returns to one sentence: "A medium declares which of the 22 verbs it carries, and at what cost."
(`doc/THE-SECOND-FOUNDING.md:309`), and its contract form, "A new medium adds renderings, never verbs."
(`conformance/violations.json:290`). Behind both: "*Because* they are closed, a coverage matrix over media is finite and
checkable." (`doc/THE-SECOND-FOUNDING.md:326-327`).

Proposed bold sentence, in the book's words: **A law can check every sign only when the signs are few, fixed, and named
in advance.** Alternative: **A vocabulary closed by law is what lets a stranger read a sign at speed, and a machine check
a declaration.** (The stake of the chapter is the author's, C10: propose, mark.)

What kind of source would carry each clause of the author's claim:

- **"closed vocabulary"**: the convention's own text and annexes (source 1 or 2): the articles that define the classes of
  signs and the annex that fixes their forms; a clause that limits signs to those forms, if the text holds one.
- **"legislated by treaty"**: the treaty record (source 1 and 4): the convention's title, place and date, its status page
  and its parties. This clause is the easiest to carry at its true size.
- **"amended on crash evidence"**: the amendment record (source 2 and 4): the dated amendments, and the WP.1 documents
  that propose them, with their stated reasons. If no amendment text cites crash or casualty data, the clause is restated
  at its true size ("amended by its parties through a standing working party").
- **"working across languages and literacy"**: the convention's stated aim or preamble, if it says so (source 1); or a
  sign-comprehension study, or a standard method for testing the comprehension of graphical symbols (ISO publishes one in
  the 9186 series, to be confirmed). A sentence of the founding document is not a source for it.
- **"for sixty years"**: the convention's date (source 1). Signed in 1968 is 58 years before the reading of 2026 by
  arithmetic; earlier international instruments on road signs may exist, and only the treaty record says which. The
  writer states the date read, not a round number.

## 8. Where the field leads

The loss beside the win, from the record's own sentences:

- The win: a closed set makes the matrix "finite and checkable" (`THE-SECOND-FOUNDING.md:326-327`). The loss: "The
  closure that looked like an aesthetic constraint" (`:328`); a closed list "goes stale -- the next channel always
  arrives" (`:333`), which is why media are classified by properties (`:335-336`); "ten is not a finished number either"
  (`:950`).
- The win: an external standard brings settled meanings. The loss: it binds Zayn absolutely; "a rule of ours assigning
  red another meaning is not a divergence, it is a hazard" (`:775-776`), and the court may only refuse a conflict, "never
  to resolve it in our favour" (`:776-777`).
- The win: one meaning per value. The loss: meaning "is a property of the sign *and its reader*" (`:367-368`); "A chapter
  on culture written by one culture is the failure it warns about" (`:963-964`). Part VI takes this up.

## 10. In your hands

**The exit sign.** A stairwell sign shows the way out. Its declaration promises that leaving stays within reach
(`exit-sign.zayn`):

```zayn
DEFINE VIBE stairwell_sign (
  SURFACE_AS signage
)

DEFINE INTENT_FLOW way_out (
  SATISFIES [DISCOVER, LOCATE],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [EXIT]
)
//--> VERB_NOT_CARRIED way_out
```
Answer: `exit-sign.zayn:5  error  VERB_NOT_CARRIED  EXIT in way_out on signage`, Rule 82, Article II. Exit 1.

What the reader then tries, each probed:

- Move `EXIT` into `SATISFIES` (`SATISFIES [DISCOVER, LOCATE, EXIT]`, no `ALWAYS_REACHABLE`): the same finding, `EXIT in
  way_out on signage`. Exit 1.
- Light the sign: `SURFACE_AS luminous` with `SATISFIES [DISCOVER, LOCATE]`: `VERB_NOT_CARRIED  LOCATE in way_out on
  luminous`. Exit 1 (a lamp carries `DISCOVER` and `UNDERSTAND` only, `media.json:88`).
- Two VIBEs in one file, `signage` and `luminous`, the flow `SATISFIES [DISCOVER, LOCATE]`: `VERB_NOT_CARRIED  LOCATE in
  way_out on luminous`. Exit 1 (every VIBE's medium is read against every declaration of the file, `zayn.js:408`, `:606-616`).
- The accepted form: `SURFACE_AS signage`, `SATISFIES [DISCOVER, LOCATE]`, no `ALWAYS_REACHABLE`: **clean**, exit 0.

The lesson the run gives: `EXIT` is the user's intent to leave a flow; a sign that shows the way out serves `LOCATE`. The
word on the sign and the verb of the law are two things.

Open question it leaves (proposed to the author, C2): the medium is declared per file, so a building's sign and its lit
companion cannot sit in one file with one flow each; and the right to leave, which the law guards everywhere else, cannot
be promised on a sign. Whether that is the medium's truth or a gap is the law's to say (request, section 12).
