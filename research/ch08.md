## What the chapter does

It makes the verifier refuse, prints the refusal as the run gave it, and reads it: code, name, sentence, then rule, article
and right. It repairs the declaration and runs it again. It shows one declaration with four findings, the case of an act
that cannot be undone, the machine form of a finding and the exit codes. Readable alone (it is on the proposed spine).

## The declarations, probed

Each was written to a file and checked. The chapter's blocks are these, and the build re-runs them.

**1. `payments.zayn`, as first written.**

```
DEFINE INTENT_FLOW approve_payment (
  SATISFIES [SELECT, CONFIRM, ACT]
)
```
```
payments.zayn:1  error  MISSING_UNDO  approve_payment
    An act declares no way back: SATISFIES holds ACT, ALWAYS_REACHABLE lacks UNDO.
    Rule 79 · The Undo Covenant · Article IV · the right to reverse
payments.zayn:1  error  MISSING_CANCEL  approve_payment
    A flow that acts cannot be abandoned: ALWAYS_REACHABLE lacks CANCEL.
    Rule 26 · The Back Button is Holy · Article IV · the right to leave

2 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 1 file(s) at level 2
```
Exit 1. It is the declaration of the Zayn site's front page (`index.html:83-93`), which reports the same two findings.

**2. The same, with `ALWAYS_REACHABLE [UNDO, CANCEL]`.** `clean — 1 file(s), 0 violations at level 2 against Zayn v3.17`. Exit 0.

**3. A wizard and a feedback, four findings** (the suite's `multiple-violations.zayn`, without its `ACTOR`): `SELECT_ACT_COLLAPSE`,
`MISSING_UNDO`, `TRAPPED_FLOW` on `bulk_delete`; `DECORATIVE_ANIMATION` on `delete_flourish`. Exit 1. Citations as printed:
Rule 16 and Article II with no right; Rule 79, Article IV, the right to reverse; Rules 51 and 53, Article II, the right to
leave; Rule 112 and Article I.

**4. `purge_archive`, `ROLE irreversible` with `ALWAYS_REACHABLE [UNDO, CANCEL]`.** One finding:
`archive.zayn:4  error  IRREVERSIBLE_CLAIMS_UNDO  irreversible purge_archive declares UNDO`, citing Rule 79, Article IV, the
right to reverse. Exit 1. The subject is a phrase here, so a promise line names the declaration and the judge looks for that
name as a word of the subject (DECISIONS.md RUN-01).

**5. The same with `ALWAYS_REACHABLE [CANCEL]`.** Clean. Exit 0.

**6. The exercise: a wizard that closes an account.**

```
DEFINE WIZARD close_account (
  SATISFIES [SELECT, ACT],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [CANCEL],
  STEPS [choose, review, close]
)
```
Three findings: `SELECT_ACT_COLLAPSE`, `MISSING_UNDO`, `TRAPPED_FLOW`. What the reader then meets, each probed:

- `ROLE irreversible` written on the wizard: `ROLE_MISPLACED` ("ROLE on WIZARD close_account; a role belongs to an INTERACTION"), citing Article II.
- The wizard keeping `ACT` with `ALWAYS_REACHABLE [CANCEL, EXIT]`, and an `INTERACTION close_for_good` beside it with
  `ROLE irreversible`: `MISSING_UNDO` on the flow all the same. The interaction's role does not reach the flow.
- The wizard with `SATISFIES [SELECT, CONFIRM]` and `ALWAYS_REACHABLE [CANCEL, EXIT]`, and the interaction with
  `SATISFIES [CONFIRM, ACT]`, `ALWAYS_REACHABLE [CANCEL]`, `ROLE irreversible`: **clean**. This is the form the verifier
  accepts for a flow whose act cannot be undone.
- The interaction pointing at the wizard with `INTENT_FLOW close_account`: a warning, `UNRESOLVED_INTENT_FLOW`
  ("close_account referenced by close_for_good"), because the reference resolves against `INTENT_FLOW` declarations and the
  flow here is a `WIZARD`. Exit 0.

So in the clean form nothing in the file says that the interaction is the wizard's last step. That is the defect left in the
reader's hands, proposed to the author (C2 makes the choice his), and filed as a finding (BOOK-ZAYN-04).

## Findings for the law and for the Zayn repository, filed as requests

- **BOOK-ZAYN-03, texts that differ from the data or the tree.** `conformance/README.md:114`, `tools/zayn/README.md:202`
  and `doc/language/README.md:159` link `doc/decisions/M3-the-zayn-verifier.md`; the file at the pin is
  `doc/decisions/M3-the-zane-verifier.md`. `tools/zayn/README.md:20` shows the citation in brackets on the finding's first
  line; the run prints it on its own line, as `VIOLATIONS.md` section 5 specifies. `tools/zayn/README.md:10` names version
  0.1.0. `conformance/README.md:46-51` gives 6, 20 and 4 fixtures per level; the manifest holds 12, 60 and 5.
- **BOOK-ZAYN-04, a flow whose act cannot be undone.** The invariant's second half ("or declares why it cannot") is
  expressible on an `INTERACTION` only. A `WIZARD` or an `INTENT_FLOW` that acts must keep `UNDO`, or move its act into an
  `INTERACTION` that no field ties back to it; a reference by `INTENT_FLOW` to a `WIZARD` does not resolve. Found by
  probing, for the docket.
- **BOOK-ZAYN-05, the tier of a rule and the diagnostics that cite it tell two stories.** The 40 diagnostics cite 14
  distinct rules. By `enforcement` in `rules.json`: 5 `machine` (38, 79, 82, 83, 130), 1 `tool` (116), 1 `machine-later`
  (26, cited by `MISSING_CANCEL`) and 7 `judgment` (16, 51, 53, 98, 112, 118, 119). Six `machine` rules are cited by no
  diagnostic (1, 44, 60, 80, 107, 127). And a reader who opens Rule 16, *Intentional Clicks*, from `SELECT_ACT_COLLAPSE`
  finds a rule about what a control looks like; the sentence the refusal enforces is Article II's ("Selection never implies
  execution"). Chapter 17 prints which rule sits on which rung, so the book needs the law's own answer first.

## After the fresh readers (2026-11-22)

Three readers who had not written the chapter read it: a verifier against the record (`research/verify/ch08.md`: 77 claims,
63 verified, 3 wrong, 3 unsupported, 8 overclaims), a voice reader (`research/voice/ch08.md`) and a designer who does not
program, reading the built page (`research/read/ch08-designer.md`: "ready after the items above"; the sentence that stopped
the reader most: "a flow of two steps has no exit"). Their reports are kept as they wrote them, on the first draft. What the
fixes changed, and what the build then proved (7 of 7 declarations, 13 of 13 fixtures, 2026-11-22 00:21):

- **Three words defined at the door**: declaration, law, verifier; and linter, exit code, build, fixture, contract, each where it
  first appears. How to run a declaration is said before the first plate.
- **"Promise" keeps one sense.** The page's check is "the answer this page expects" (the proof line, the verdict line, the prose);
  "promise" is left to what a declaration promises its user.
- **Plate 8.3 is read finding by finding**, with the field each one read; `EXIT` is told apart from `CANCEL`; the fourth finding
  is said to stand on a tone with no `REPORTS`. Its declarations are now the fixture's own (`ACTOR operator` restored), and its
  repair is a plate of its own (8.4, clean).
- **The exercise's accepted form is a run block** (plate 8.8, clean), placed after "Try before you read on."
- **Narrowed**: the principle (what the law holds for a code, since half the codes bind no rule and some no right); the suite
  compares codes, and nothing is said of two verifiers "written apart" (the binary is a port held on order, spans and messages,
  `doc/design/BINARY.md:123-126`); exit code 0 is "refused nothing" (a run with warnings only exits 0); the twins are said where
  the suite has them; axe-core's rules are said with "impact and tags", which is what the register records.
- **Cut**: the refusal "two findings for one defect", which no plate of this chapter runs; it belongs to the chapter on roles.
- **A sentence of the record is now quoted as the record's** ("undo reverses what was done, cancel abandons what is being done",
  `doc/language/VIOLATIONS.md`), where the draft had it as prose.
- **One sentence is an interpretation and is flagged for the author**: "The verb `CONFIRM` names the user's intent to commit,
  whatever control carries it." The record says verbs are intents and that a declaration never says how a surface looks; it does
  not say this sentence. The designer asked which the law wants, since Rule 79's reason speaks against confirmation while
  `SELECT_ACT_COLLAPSE` asks for `CONFIRM` (added to BOOK-ZAYN-05).

Added to **BOOK-ZAYN-03**: `conformance/README.md:36` says the id "carries the rule number"; the contract binds a rule to 20 of
