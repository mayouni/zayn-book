## 1. What the chapter does

It teaches a method a team can use with no tool installed: how a defect a person saw becomes a rule that holds the next
time. It walks seven steps, each on a real record of the Zayn repository: write the correction down in the person's words
with its place and its date; check whether the law already holds it; write the rule in the law's shape; decide which
instrument can decide it and file its tier; place it on the grid; turn it into a check that fails on the page as it was
and passes on the page as corrected; amend what the rule touches. The correction it follows end to end is the menu remark
of 2026-11-08, which amended Rule 116 and gave the contract three diagnostics with fixtures. It ends on a template a
reader fills. It closes Part VII: chapter 18 has told how the law is amended; this chapter gives the reader the pen.
