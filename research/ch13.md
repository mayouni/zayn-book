## 1. What the chapter does

It sets out the grid on which Zayn places its law: six activities of the user (perceive, understand, decide, act, recover,
trust) crossed with the ten media of `media.json` and one more column, `any`, the law stated before it is projected into a
medium. It says which cells are closed and why, which hold a rule and which are empty, and how a rule is placed: its activities
read from its title and reason, its medium from its own words, by the author's rulings. It runs the projection fixtures, which
apply an existing rule under a declared medium, and one declaration of its own that lands on an empty cell. The empty cells
are the argument (the contract, line 166): told as where the law reaches by projection and where its next rules will be placed.

## 4. The principle

its ground in the design document: reach is not counted as coverage (`COVERAGE.md:122-125`), and "The honest gap did not move,

Proposed bold sentence, in the book's words (affirmative, the empty cell as stage and direction):

**A cell is held when a rule is written for it, so every empty cell shows where the law reaches by projection and where its
next rule will stand.**
