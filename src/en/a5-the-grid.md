This appendix gives the data the grid of chapter 13 is counted from. It holds the {{n:grid.activities}} activities of the user and the verbs each owns, and the {{n:media.total}} media with the verbs each carries at a cost or does not carry. Then it gives the grid itself, the media crossed with the activities. Here a reader finds every verb with its activity and every medium with its verbs.

The activities, and the verbs each owns.

{{acts}}

The media, and what each does with the law's verbs.

{{media}}

The verbs each medium carries at a cost, and those it does not carry. Only a verb a medium does not carry raises a finding, `VERB_NOT_CARRIED`. It does so when a file names the medium and one of its declarations promises the verb.

{{carriage}}

The grid: for each medium and each activity, the number of rules placed on the cell, or closed when the medium carries none of the activity's verbs.

{{grid}}
