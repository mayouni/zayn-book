This appendix prints the law as its data holds it at version {{n:law.version}}: the preamble, the {{n:law.articles}} articles, the {{n:law.rights}} rights of the user, the {{n:law.forbidden}} forbidden patterns, then the {{n:law.sets}} sets and their {{n:law.rules}} rules. A rule's card gives its text, its article, its tier, what it tells its reader to avoid, and its reason. The last table lists the numbers that the law keeps out of use, which is why the rules are numbered up to {{n:law.highest}}.

The law's file is the authority, and it is public with Zayn. Where its text names a product of the estate, this copy gives a description in square brackets.
