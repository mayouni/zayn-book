This appendix lists the {{n:diag.total}} kinds of finding of the verifier's contract, by level: the code, the severity, the contract's own sentence, and what the contract binds the code to in the law. An error is a refusal; a warning is a finding that lets the declaration pass. Chapter 9 gives the four levels.
