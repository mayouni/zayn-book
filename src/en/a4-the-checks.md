This appendix lists the {{n:audit.checks}} numbered checks of Zayn's design system: what each reads, when it fails, and the causes of the corrections it answers. The audit of the painted page is a page of Zayn's repository that opens another page in a browser and measures what is drawn. It runs {{n:audit.paint}} of the checks on the painted page, at {{n:audit.widths}} widths and in both of the site's cultures. The other {{n:audit.files}} read the site's files, in the tool that builds the site. Chapter 10 groups them by what they read, and chapter 17 tells their recorded runs.
